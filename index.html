Privacy Policy

Bingo Classic Game
Effective date: 11 November 2026
Developer: Logk ("we", "us")

1. Overview

Bingo Classic Game ("the app") is a Bingo game you can play offline against computer players or online with friends. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and what choices you have. The app has no user accounts. We do not sell the information described in sections 2 and 3.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

  • Your nickname and chosen avatar.
  • Your birth date, entered on the first-launch screen. It is used only on your device to decide which ad settings apply. We never send the birth date to our servers or to any third party. Only the resulting ad settings (for example "treat as child-directed" or "non-personalized ads") are passed to the ad software.
  • Your settings (sound, vibration), a flag that the how-to guide has been shown, ad frequency counters, and whether you have bought "Remove ads".
  • Temporary information that lets you rejoin an online game after the app was closed: the room code, a random private connection ID and your board.
  • A flag that you have seen the online-safety reminder.

3. Information sent when you play online

Playing offline against computer players sends nothing to our server. To play online, the app connects to our game server, hosted on Cloudflare. It sends your nickname, your avatar, a random temporary connection ID, the room code, and your game moves (the numbers you call and how many lines you have completed). When a game ends, your board is shown to the other players in the room so that everyone can see the result.

We use this information only to run the game. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location. Please use a nickname instead of your real name. Room data is held only while a room is in use. It is deleted as soon as everyone has left a room, after about 2 minutes if nobody ever joins, and in any case after about 10 minutes without activity. Our own server logs record technical events such as room codes and counts, not nicknames or boards. Cloudflare, our hosting provider, processes IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the service, under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

If you open a room invite link, the page at bingo.logk.in is a static page that only shows the room code and links to the app and Google Play. We do not add cookies or trackers to it. The service that hosts it may log your IP address in the normal way.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The AdMob software may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location) and information about how you interact with ads and the app, to show and measure ads. How ads are configured depends on the birth date you enter:

AgeAds
Under 13Only banner ads are shown (no full-screen ads). Ads are treated as child-directed, are not personalized and are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
13 to 17Ads are not personalized and are limited to mild content.
18 and overAds may be personalized. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) we ask for your consent first through Google's consent form, which you can open again from Settings.

Until a birth date has been entered, no ads are requested. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

You can make a one-time purchase to remove ads. Payment is handled by Google Play and we never see your payment details. For players aged 13 and over, the app uses RevenueCat to check and restore your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information. For players under 13 the app does not offer purchases and does not start this service. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. Children

The app asks for a birth date so that ad settings can match the player's age. For players under 13, ads are limited as described in section 4 and purchases are switched off. The only personal information a child can enter is a nickname, which should not be a real name. We do not collect contact details. If you believe a child has provided personal information that you want removed, contact us and we will help.

Players under 13 cannot type a nickname. The app generates one from a list (for example "Happy Panda 42") and shows a short online-safety reminder before their first online game. Until a birth date has been entered, the app treats a player the same way for online play.

7. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Online game rooms: deleted when everyone has left, after about 2 minutes if nobody joins, and after about 10 minutes without activity.
  • Information held by Google, RevenueCat and Cloudflare is kept according to their own policies.

8. Security

Connections between the app and our game server are encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International use

The app is available worldwide. Our providers may process information in countries other than yours.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or US state privacy laws). We hold very little information that can be linked to you. To make a request or ask a question, contact us using the address below.
