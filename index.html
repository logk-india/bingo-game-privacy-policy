Privacy Policy

Bingo Classic Game
Developer: Logk
Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Logk ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Bingo Classic Game (the "Game"). The Game includes offline play against computer-controlled players and online multiplayer rooms.

In short: Your profile name, avatar, age and game board are saved on your device. When you join an online multiplayer room, your player name, avatar and game board are shared with the other players in that room so the multiplayer game can work. Your exact age is not sent to Google AdMob.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the Game, the following information may be handled:

  • Player profile: The player name or nickname and the avatar you select.
  • Age: The age you enter in the Game. It is stored locally on your device and used by the Game to determine the appropriate advertising request configuration.
  • Game board and game activity: Your 5×5 board, selected numbers, called-number/game progress and related room gameplay information.
  • Online room information: Room code, player/room identifiers, player connection state, turn and game state information needed to operate multiplayer games.
  • Technical and connection information: Information that may be processed when your device connects to our online multiplayer service, such as network connection data, IP address, timestamps and service/security logs.
  • Purchase information: Information made available by the relevant app store or billing provider to process, verify or restore an in-app purchase. We do not ask you to provide your full payment card details in the Game.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores your player name, selected avatar, age and game data locally on your device so that your profile and game can be maintained between sessions. You can clear locally stored Game data by using the device or app controls available to you; uninstalling the Game may also remove locally stored data, depending on your device settings and backup configuration.

3. Online Multiplayer and Information Shared in a Room

If you use online multiplayer, information needed for the room is transmitted to our multiplayer service. Your player name, avatar and game board are shared with the other players in the same room. This sharing is necessary for the multiplayer experience and allows players to see the room participants and play the game.

Do not use your full legal name or include contact details, passwords, or other sensitive information in your player name. Only join rooms with people you choose to play with, and remember that information shown to room participants can be seen by those participants.

4. Multiplayer Service Provider – Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects to support online multiplayer rooms, player connections, room state and game communication. When you use online multiplayer, information is processed by Cloudflare to provide and secure that service. Cloudflare may also process technical data, such as IP addresses and connection or security logs, in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Cloudflare privacy information: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5. Advertising – Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app/device information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information, subject to the applicable Google services, settings and consent requirements.

The Game checks the age entered by the player on the device and uses the resulting age category to configure the advertising request appropriately. The player's exact age is stored locally and is not sent to Google AdMob by the Game. This does not mean that AdMob receives no information: the AdMob SDK and its partners may process technical and advertising-related information as described in Google's documentation.

Google advertising and privacy information: Google – Advertising · Google Privacy Policy

6. In-App Purchases

The Game offers in-app purchases. Purchases are handled through the billing service provided by the platform on which you download the Game (for example, Google Play Billing on Android). The platform may process payment details, purchase history and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive purchase-related information, such as product, transaction or entitlement status, to provide, verify or restore purchased features. We do not receive or store your full payment card number through the Game.

For Google Play information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics

We do not currently integrate a third-party analytics SDK in the Game. Our multiplayer infrastructure provider may process technical or operational logs as part of providing and securing the online service.

8. How We Use Information

We use information handled by the Game to:

  • Save and display your player profile and game progress;
  • create, operate and reconnect online multiplayer rooms;
  • share the information required for gameplay with other players in your room;
  • configure advertising requests based on the age category determined on your device;
  • process, verify and restore in-app purchases; and
  • maintain the security, reliability and proper operation of the Game.

9. Children’s Privacy

Bingo Classic Game is intended for a general audience and may be used by children under 13. We ask for an age so the Game can determine the appropriate advertising treatment on the device. The exact age is stored locally and is not sent to AdMob by the Game.

Because the Game supports online multiplayer, a player's name, avatar and board may be visible to other people in the same room. Parents and guardians should help children choose a nickname that does not reveal their real-world identity and should supervise children's use of online rooms and purchases.

We do not intend to collect more personal information from children than is reasonably needed to operate the Game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us at the address below. We will review the request and take appropriate steps where required by applicable law.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Profile, age and game data saved locally remain on your device until they are changed or deleted through the Game/device, or the app's local data is removed. Online room and connection information is handled for as long as needed to operate the room, support reconnection and maintain service security. Technical logs may be retained by service providers according to their operational, security and legal requirements. We do not state a fixed retention period because it may depend on the service and the type of information.

To ask about information associated with your use of the Game, or to request deletion where applicable, contact us using the details in the Contact section. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction purposes.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your Choices

  • You can choose the player name and avatar used in the Game.
  • You can choose whether to use offline play or join an online room.
  • You can stop using online multiplayer at any time.
  • You can manage advertising/privacy choices through options made available by the app, your device or the relevant platform, where available.
  • You can manage or restrict in-app purchases through your platform's device or account settings.

13. Third-Party Services and Links

The Game relies on third-party services, including Google AdMob, the relevant app store billing service and Cloudflare for online multiplayer. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies. Their practices are not controlled by this Privacy Policy.

14. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Where this happens, the information may be subject to the laws of those locations, subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy
